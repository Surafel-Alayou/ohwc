# One Heart Wholeness Centre (OHWC) Website

## Overview

The **One Heart Wholeness Centre (OHWC) Website** is an online platform developed to strengthen the organisation's digital presence, communicate its mission, and raise awareness of its work supporting children with disabilities in Ethiopia.

Based in Bahir Dar, the centre provides individualised support and therapeutic activities for children, including those with autism. The website introduces the organisation, showcases its activities, shares stories of the children it supports, and connects visitors, volunteers, and potential donors with opportunities to get involved.

## Key Features

- Information about the organisation, its mission, and activities.
- Stories and profiles of children supported by the centre.
- Updates on events, news, and community activities.
- A photo gallery showcasing the centre's work.
- Information on donations and opportunities to support the organisation.
- Contact information for visitors and potential partners.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- **Framework:** Bootstrap
- **Libraries:** AOS, Swiper and GLightbox

## Purpose and Impact

The website helps increase awareness of OHWC's work, improve communication with the wider community, and encourage support for its mission of helping children with disabilities develop their abilities and reach their potential.
