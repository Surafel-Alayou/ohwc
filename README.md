# One Heart Wholeness Centre (OHWC) Website

## Overview

The **One Heart Wholeness Centre (OHWC) Website** is an online platform developed to introduce the organisation, communicate its mission, and raise awareness of its work supporting children with disabilities in Ethiopia.

Based in Bahir Dar, the centre provides individualised support and therapeutic activities for children with different disabilities, including autism. The website serves as a central platform where visitors, supporters, volunteers, and potential donors can learn about the organisation, explore its activities, and discover ways to contribute.

## Main Features

- **About Us:** Introduces the organisation, its background, mission, and objectives.
- **The OHWC:** Explains the centre's work, services, and approach to supporting children.
- **Autistic Unit:** Highlights activities and individualised support for children with autism.
- **Meet Our Children:** Shares children's stories and introduces those supported by the centre.
- **Events and News:** Provides updates on activities, events, and organisational developments.
- **Gallery:** Showcases photographs of the centre, its children, and community activities.
- **Donations:** Provides information on how individuals and organisations can support the centre.
- **Contact:** Offers communication details for visitors, supporters, and potential partners.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- **Framework / UI:** Bootstrap
- **Libraries:** AOS, Swiper, GLightbox and Font Awesome

## Purpose and Impact

The website was developed to strengthen OHWC's online presence, increase public awareness, and connect the organisation with a wider community. By presenting its work, sharing the children's stories, and providing donation information, the platform helps encourage engagement and support for the centre's mission.
